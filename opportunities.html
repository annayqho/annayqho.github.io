Undergraduate Students

Research positions in my group are full for the 2026-27 academic year. Right now, the only way to join the group is to apply to the Nexus Scholars program (open to Cornell students in any year; applications open in late October) or the Cornell Astronomy REU program (open to non-Cornell students who are US citizens or permanent residents and have completed at least one year of undergraduate study; the deadline for Summer 2026 was January 29, 2026).

There are many summer research opportunities in astronomy. For US citizens, the NSF-funded REU program list is a great place to start. For non-US citizens, a list of opportunities can be found here. Another option open to US and non-US citizens is the Caltech SURF program. Other opportunities for undergraduate students at Cornell include the Rawlings CPRS program (open to incoming students and sophomores; nominations due in February), the McNair Scholars program, and the Humanities Scholars Program (deadline spring of sophomore year). Post-bac opportunities include the CRESST II program at NASA GSFC, SPARK

Graduate Students

I am accepting new PhD students this year. Please apply directly to the Cornell Astronomy PhD program, and please do not email me directly. I receive more emails from prospective students than I can answer.

Fellowship opportunities include the NY Space Grant (due July), the NSF GRFP (due October), NASA FINESST, and the DOE Computational Science Graduate Fellowship. Post-graduation opportunities include the NY State Science Policy Fellowship.
